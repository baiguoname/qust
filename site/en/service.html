qust Services and Source Delivery

Services around high-performance operator development, quantitative platform engineering, source-code delivery, private deployment, data-source integration, and trading-system integration.

Core Services High-performance operator development Quantitative platform engineering Source delivery and private deployment
01

High-performance operators

Move Python research logic into Rust/Arrow execution paths for rolling, rank, group, over, tick/kline, backtesting, risk-control, and trading-state workloads.

  • Define input/output schema, null behavior, and streaming semantics.
  • Implement Rust native executors, Python namespaces, and examples.
  • Add focused tests for small samples, batches, streaming, and performance.
02

Quant platform engineering

Build a unified workflow around market data, factors, strategies, backtests, parameters, monitoring, and reports.

  • Design data ingestion, caching, chunked execution, and result archives.
  • Establish strategy templates, indicator libraries, evaluation, and portfolio analysis.
  • Organize notebooks, scripts, services, and monitoring pages into one workflow.
03

Source and private deployment

Deliver source code, build scripts, deployment notes, and extension conventions for private long-term maintenance.

  • Support closed-source delivery, version upgrades, and stable interfaces.
  • Provide Python/Rust build, wheel, Docker, and CI guidance.
  • Adapt deployment to team permissions, data security, and operations.
04

Data sources and remote compute

Connect Parquet, ClickHouse, LazyFrame, streaming data, and custom datasources with fewer IO branches in research code.

  • ClickHouse schema, Date/DateTime, string, and Arrow type adaptation.
  • Large-data chunked reads, predicate pushdown, and streaming execution chains.
  • Data-quality checks, sampling validation, and error localization.
05

Monitor and research UI

Turn price, signals, PnL, distributions, parameters, debug traces, and callbacks into pages researchers can use directly.

  • Customize charts, linked selection, parameter panels, and debug views.
  • Turn notebook outputs into stable research dashboards.
  • Keep expression logic connected to the visualization layer.
06

Trading integration

Connect offline expressions to simulated or live trading APIs, with attention to signal timing, positions, risk controls, and replayable logs.

  • Keep research expressions, backtests, simulations, and live calls aligned.
  • Integrate trading APIs such as QMT with separate API and strategy layers.
  • Provide runbooks, logs, pause/stop, and recovery mechanisms.
